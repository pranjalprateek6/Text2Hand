What it does

A hand, not a font

01

Text becomes pages

Headings, lists, quotes and tables come out the way a person would write them, with tables ruled by hand. Long documents spill onto new pages, up to eighty at a time, and the first page appears while the rest are written.

02

PDFs come along

Drop a PDF and its text lands in the editor, so you can fix anything before a word is written. Two-column papers are read down each column, and scanned pages are read too.

03

Equations survive

Equations are lifted straight from the paper and traced onto the page as ink, fractions intact. Greek letters and maths symbols are written by the same hand: α β ψ φ Σ and friends.

04

A look for every page

Notebook, plain sheet, letter, clean scan or lab notes, in one of four pens and three sizes, on A4 or Letter. Or no paper at all: the ink alone as a transparent PNG, ready to drop into a design.

For developers: capture your own hand with the printable sheets and a phone photo.

How it works

Three steps to paper

I

Type, paste or drop a PDF

Notes, a letter, a whole paper. A PDF's text lands in the editor first, so anything the reading got wrong is a quick fix.

II

Pick a look

Notebook, plain sheet, letter, clean scan or lab notes, any of four pens. The page updates as you type, beside the text.

III

Download

The document as a PDF, any page as a PNG (transparent if you like), or every page zipped. A document keeps its handwriting from one render to the next; New handwriting deals a fresh one.

The hand

No two letters the same

This page was written from a few lines of typed notes. Every mark on it comes from one real hand, captured from written sheets and cut apart, letter by letter and word by word.

01
Variants and jitterEach letter gets its own rotation, size and wobble, and each word its own pen pressure. Repeats on a page never match.
02
Whole words in one strokeCommon words are single captured strokes, keeping the joins a real hand makes.
03
A hand that tiresWriting grows a little larger and looser as a long document goes on, the way a hand does over a sitting.
04
Paper that was scannedRuled, squared or plain paper with warm grain and a slight tilt, or no paper at all.
05
The same hand each timeA document keeps its handwriting from one render to the next, so fixing a typo does not rewrite the whole thing in a new hand.
